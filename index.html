Why Choose Japan for Higher Education?

Japan is world-renowned for its top-tier educational standards, safe living environment, rich cultural heritage, and incredible career opportunities for international students.

Safe & Welcoming Environment

Consistently ranked among the safest countries globally with high quality of life.

28 Hours/Week Part-Time Work

Students holding a student visa are permitted to work up to 28 hours per week during academic terms.

Global Educational Standards

State-of-the-art facilities, innovative technology, and internationally recognized degrees.

Intake Seasons in Japan

Japanese language schools and universities accept applications for 4 main intake seasons each year:

April Intake 2-Year Course (Major Intake)
October Intake 1.5-Year Course
July Intake 1.9-Year Course
January Intake 1.3-Year Course

Plan ahead! Applications open 6 to 8 months prior to intake.
